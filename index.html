Trading Card Price Prediction

An end-to-end ML pipeline predicting Pokémon card prices from intrinsic card features and extrinsic market signals, built on ~34K cleaned cards from 40K+ raw records. I taught a Random Forest what a Charizard is worth. It’s very opinionated now.

  • Benchmarked Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Linear Regression and MLP, with R² up to 0.876
